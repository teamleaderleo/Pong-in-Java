Physics personality

Integer coordinates. Collision checks after movement. Secret dead lines. Bonus movement during bounces. Full bullshit telemetry enabled.

What gets counted

Harmful: paddle and blocker phase-throughs. These build the combo and receive a freeze-frame review.

Weird saves: broken reflections that send the ball back into play.

Nudges: oversized visible movement from bonus moves. They stay separate from phase-throughs.

BS-assisted goals: points scored shortly after a harmful chain.
